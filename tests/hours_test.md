## Choose a long service
Tick only "Full colour" (120 minutes). Click "Next: stylist & time".

## Check the last start time on a Tuesday
Keep "Any stylist". Choose a date that is a Tuesday at least 3 days from today. Look at the list of start times.
Tuesday hours are 10:00 to 18:00, so the LAST start time offered must be 16:00 and 16:15 must not be offered. The first must be 10:00.

## Check a Monday
Change the date to a Monday at least 3 days from today. The page says the salon is closed and shows no start times.

## Check a Friday
Change the date to a Friday at least 3 days from today. Friday hours are 10:00 to 20:00, so the last start time must be 18:00.
